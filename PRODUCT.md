# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who use Slack on macOS and want their status to reflect workdays, meetings, media playback, and deliberate manual actions without recurring maintenance.

## Product Purpose

Slack Status Bot keeps Slack status and presence accurate through automation while preserving explicit user choices. Success means the app quietly does the right thing and makes configuration understandable when preferences need to change.

## Brand Personality

Pragmatic, direct, unobtrusive.

## Anti-references

Decorative dashboards, nested cards, pill-heavy controls, verbose guidance, and unfamiliar replacements for standard form controls.

## Design Principles

- Put the current task ahead of decoration.
- Keep settings compact and immediately understandable.
- Prefer familiar platform controls and existing interaction patterns.
- Make automation observable without demanding attention.
- Protect deliberate user choices from automatic changes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast and keyboard access expectations. Do not rely on color alone, respect reduced-motion preferences, and keep controls clearly labelled.
